Flagship Initiative

GIRL GEEKS[cite: 1]

EMPOWERING YOUNG WOMEN IN TECHNOLOGY[cite: 1]

A flagship initiative by IEEE Computer Society Bangalore Chapter[cite: 1].

About Girl Geeks: A platform that empowers young women to ideate, innovate and build technology-driven solutions to real-world problems through workshops, mentorship and a structured innovation journey[cite: 1].

Team Requirements[cite: 1]
  • Minimum 3 members, Maximum 5 members[cite: 1].
  • Minimum 50% girls[cite: 1].
  • At least 1 IEEE member, with at least 1 CS member[cite: 1].
Competition Phase[cite: 1]
  • Design (3 Weeks): Design your solution with guidance and mentorship[cite: 1].
  • Prototype (1 Week): Build your prototype and test your idea[cite: 1].
